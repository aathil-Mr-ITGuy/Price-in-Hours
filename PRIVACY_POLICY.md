# Privacy Policy for Price in Hours

**Last Updated: February 6, 2026**

## Overview

Price in Hours is a browser extension that helps you understand product prices in terms of hours worked. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any personal data.**

All your information stays on your device:

- Salary settings
- Currency preferences
- Work hours configuration

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. This data:

- Never leaves your browser
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not shared with any third parties

## Permissions

The extension requires these permissions:

- **storage**: To save your salary settings locally
- **activeTab**: To display price badges on e-commerce websites

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/aathil-Mr-ITGuy/Price-in-Hours).
